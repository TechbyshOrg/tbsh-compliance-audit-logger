Prepared 2026-11-25 for version 2.0.0. Internal notes; excluded from the release package.

Estimates are marked "est.". There was no keyword-volume tool; WordPress.org figures
come from the public plugin API on 2026-11-25.


1. POSITIONING
--------------
One sentence (use it everywhere):
  Compliance Audit Log is a tamper-evident audit log for WordPress that records who
  changed what and proves the record has not been edited.

Display name:      Compliance Audit Log – Tamper-Evident Activity Log
Short name:        Compliance Audit Log
Slug (unchanged):  tbsh-compliance-audit-logger
Menu (unchanged):  Compliance Logs

Main differentiators (no competitor highlights these):
  - SHA-256 hash chain with a verifier: proves the log was not changed.
  - NIS2 Article 23 incident deadlines (24 h / 72 h / 1 month).
  - Signed, verifiable compliance reports (PDF, HTML, CSV, JSON).
  - Encrypted off-site evidence copies (S3-compatible, FTP/FTPS, another site).
  - Read-only, expiring auditor links (no WordPress account needed).
  - AI agent attribution (person / AI agent / integration / automation / visitor).
  - Everything free: sessions, alerts, SIEM forwarding and external storage are
    paid features in several competing plugins.
